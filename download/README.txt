BANGLADESH — HYPER DECK (CINEMATIC MOTION EDITION)
Ten slides about Bangladesh: the delta, 1971, people, culture, economy,
nature, food, resilience and Smart Bangladesh 2041.

WHAT'S IN THIS ZIP
  Bangladesh-Hyper-Deck.html   Fully self-contained animated deck. Double-click
                               to open offline in any modern browser. No server,
                               no internet connection, no install needed.
  Bangladesh-Hyper-Deck.pdf     Ten 16:9 pages for printing or sharing.
  slides/slide-01.jpg ... 10   Individual slide images for reuse.

NINE SLIDE TRANSITIONS
  AUTO assigns a different one to every slide: dolly zoom, diagonal slice,
  iris, cube 3D, warp streak, parallax push, mosaic, shutter, flip.
  Use the TRANSITION picker in the top bar to force any single style.
  Each slide also has its own choreography: kinetic word-by-word headlines,
  3D card flips, drawn SVG rivers, clip-path reveals, elastic chips,
  count-up statistics, growing charts, spring flag reveal and Ken Burns
  photography with depth vignettes, glint lines and chromatic split.

CONTROLS (HTML deck)
  Arrow keys / Space / scroll / swipe / dots  navigate
  P  autoplay        F  fullscreen        M  motion on/off
  Home / End  first / last slide
  Reduced-motion preferences are respected automatically; Motion OFF renders
  every slide fully readable with no animation at all.
